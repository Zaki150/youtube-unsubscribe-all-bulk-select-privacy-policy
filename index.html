Privacy Policy

Youtube Unsubscribe All, Bulk & Select

Effective 26 November 2026

This page explains, in plain words, what the Extension reads, what stays on your device and what is shared.

1Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Youtube Unsubscribe All, Bulk & Select (“the Extension”) handles your information. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean the developer of the Extension.

The Extension helps you unsubscribe from YouTube channels on your own channel list page, either all at once or only the channels you pick. It works inside your browser. We never receive your YouTube data, and we never sell it.

2Information we access

  • Your subscribed channels: the details shown on your channel list page, such as channel name, handle, photo, link and subscriber count, so you can pick channels, protect channels and see what was removed.
  • Your YouTube tab: the Extension works in the YouTube tab where you are already signed in, and uses YouTube’s own buttons to unsubscribe from the channels you choose. It never asks for or saves your password.
  • License key: if you buy Premium, the key you enter is used only to check your purchase.

3How we use information

  • To unsubscribe from all channels, or only the ones you pick, and always skip your protected channels.
  • To follow the speed you choose, and let you pause and resume a run.
  • To keep an activity log of processed channels, with name, handle, result and time, so you can review it and export it as CSV, JSON or HTML.
  • To remember your settings, count your free unsubscribes and check your license.

4Where your data is kept

Your settings, protected channels, run progress, activity log and license status are saved only in your browser, on your own device. None of it is sent to us or anyone else.

Files you export are saved on your device and stay under your control. You can clear the activity log at any time from the Data tab, and removing the Extension deletes its saved data.

Your free-use counter is also saved in Chrome’s synced storage. It is only a number, with no YouTube data, and it may stay after you remove the Extension.

5What we share

We do not sell or share your data. The Extension only connects to these services, and only for the reasons below:

  • YouTube (youtube.com): to show your channel list and unsubscribe from the channels you choose, using your own session.
  • Gumroad, our payment provider: when you activate Premium, and when your browser starts after that, your license key and the product ID are sent only to check that your license is valid. No YouTube data is included.

Issue reports: “Report an issue” opens a pre-filled email in Gmail or your own email app. If you include diagnostics, they cover the Extension version, browser, language, the current page and run status, and how many channels are shown, selected and protected, never channel names. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

If you buy Premium, the payment happens on Gumroad’s website, under Gumroad’s own terms.

6Our commitments

  • We never sell or rent your data.
  • We never use your data for ads or credit decisions.
  • We use your data only to help you manage your YouTube subscriptions.
  • We never add tracking, analytics or outside code.

7Children

The Extension is not intended for children.

8Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
